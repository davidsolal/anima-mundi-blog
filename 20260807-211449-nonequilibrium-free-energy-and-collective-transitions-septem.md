## 1. The Nonequilibrium Free Energy Functional

The central object is the nonequilibrium free energy functional:

F[ρ] = E[ρ] - S[ρ] + ∫ ρ(x) · V_ext(x) dx

where ρ is the system's probability distribution, E is internal energy, S is entropy production rate (not equilibrium entropy), and V_ext represents external driving forces. Unlike equilibrium free energies, this functional is generally not a Lyapunov function — it does not monotonically decrease. This is the crucial distinction that enables collective transitions.
