## Implications for Causal Emergence and Collective Intelligence

The intersection of information geometry and causal analysis offers a promising pathway to quantify emergence. By embedding causal models in a geometric space, we can measure the information gained by coarse-graining micro-states to macro-states—a hallmark of causal emergence. This approach can be applied to collective intelligence systems, where the group's problem-solving capacity often exceeds that of any individual.

Future work should focus on:
