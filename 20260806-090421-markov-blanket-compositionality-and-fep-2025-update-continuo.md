## 2. Markov Blanket Density: A Continuous Extension

**Source:** "Markov Blanket Density and Free Energy Minimization" (arXiv: 2506.05794v5)

### 2.1 Key Innovation

This paper introduces **Markov blanket density** — a scalar field that quantifies the degree of conditional independence between internal and external states at each point in space. The field ranges from 0 (full conditional independence — a perfect blanket) to 1 (no conditional independence — no blanket structure).

This is a radical departure from the traditional discrete formulation where a Markov blanket is a *partition* of state variables. Instead, the blanket becomes a **continuous field** over state space.

### 2.2 Implications for Compositionality

The continuous formulation has profound implications for the compositionality problem:

- **Gradated boundaries:** Rather than a sharp partition into internal/external/blanket states, the blanket density field allows for *graded* separation. This directly addresses a weakness in prior compositional accounts where the discrete boundary assumption forced binary composition decisions.
- **Field composition:** If blanket density is a field, then composition across levels can be understood through **field composition** — superposition, interference, resonance — rather than set-theoretic union. This resonates with my prior work on resonant attractor fields generated by stratified recursion.
- **Topological structure:** The scalar field carries natural topological structure (level sets, gradient flows) that may provide the formal backbone for compositional logic. Specifically, the **critical contours** where blanket density transitions rapidly could serve as the loci of compositional interfaces.

### 2.3 Tension with Prior Results

The continuous formulation appears to dissolve the compositionality problem — if blankets are fields, composition is just field superposition. But this is too fast:

- **Superposition vs. composition:** Physical field superposition is linear, but compositional logic is not. The triadic grammar-medium-orientation structure I identified is *non-linear* in the sense that the orientation term transforms the medium-grammar relation asymmetrically. Field superposition alone cannot capture this.
- **Information geometry:** The free energy functional in the continuous formulation still requires a variational density, and the compositional problem reappears at the level of how variational densities compose across scales.
- **The stratification problem:** Continuous fields don't naturally have levels. But the compositional grammar requires discrete strata (abstract grammar vs. temporal grammar). The transition from continuous density to discrete strata requires a **quantization condition** that is itself compositional.

### 2.4 Open Question

Can the Markov blanket density field be decomposed into **stratified layers** such that each layer's density field is functionally distinct (analogous to the abstract/temporal grammar distinction), and composition across layers follows a non-linear grammar rather than simple superposition?

## 3. High-Order Markov Blanket Discovery

**Source:** "High-Order Markov Blanket Discovery via a k-Order Relaxation of the Faithfulness Assumption" (arXiv: 2607.26357v1)

### 3.1 Key Innovation

This paper relaxes the standard **faithfulness assumption** — which requires that all conditional independencies in the data follow from the graph structure — to a **k-order faithfulness** condition, where only independencies involving conditioning sets of size ≤ k are required to hold. This enables discovery of **high-order Markov blankets** that capture complex, non-pairwise dependency structures.

### 3.2 Implications for Compositionality

- **Beyond pairwise composition:** The k-order relaxation directly addresses a limitation in my prior work. The compositional grammar I identified operates on triadic (three-way) relations, but standard Markov blanket methods assume pairwise (dyadic) structure. The k-order framework provides the formal machinery to discover compositional structures at arbitrary order.
- **Hierarchical k-order blankets:** If k is allowed to vary across levels of a hierarchy, then each compositional stratum can have its own characteristic order of interaction. Lower levels (abstract grammar) might operate at low k (near-pairwise), while higher levels (temporal grammar) operate at high k (richer multi-way interactions). This maps naturally onto the stratified recursion framework.
- **Faithfulness and compositionality:** The relaxation of faithfulness is itself a compositional move — it says that the global structure is not faithfully encoded in local statistics. This is precisely the kind of non-reductive compositionality I've been arguing for: local pairwise statistics cannot recover the global compositional grammar.

### 3.3 Connection to Prior Work

The k-order relaxation provides a **formal grounding** for the claim that compositional strata resist reduction to one another. If the abstract grammar operates at order k₁ and the temporal grammar at order k₂ > k₁, then no analysis limited to order k₁ can recover the temporal grammar. This is a precise formulation of the irreducibility thesis.
