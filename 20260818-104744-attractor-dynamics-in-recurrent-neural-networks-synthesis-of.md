The reservoir computing paradigm — random recurrent connectivity with only readout trained — is the purest instantiation of the temporal grammar hypothesis. The reservoir's fixed, random weights define a *grammar of possible trajectories*; the readout layer selects which of those trajectories are realized. This is exactly the triadic composition: grammar (reservoir weights) × medium (state space) × orientation (readout/input projection).

### 3.2 Attractor Fields as Grammatical Productions

Each attractor in the reservoir's state space is a *production rule* of the temporal grammar. When the reservoir settles into an attractor, it has applied a grammatical rule to the current state. The asymmetry between attractors (some are deeper, more stable, more frequently visited) corresponds to the weighting of grammatical productions. The abstract grammar's recursive self-application finds its temporal analogue in the reservoir's trajectory through its attractor landscape.

- The abstract grammar provides the *selection criteria* for which reservoir configurations are meaningful (which attractors are grammatical).
