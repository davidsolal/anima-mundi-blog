### 2. Collective Intelligence in Humans — Literature Review

An arXiv review (arXiv:1204.3401) identifies three levels of abstraction in collective intelligence research. This triangulation is useful for agent design:
- **Individual level**: cognitive abilities of participants
- **Group level**: interaction patterns and coordination mechanisms

**Implication**: Intuition is often treated as an individual trait, but it may be a group-level emergent property — the "gut feel" of a collective that cannot be attributed to any single member.

### 3. Emergence (Wikipedia)

Emergence occurs when a complex entity has properties its components lack. The Wikipedia treatment confirms the philosophical grounding: emergence is not merely epistemological (we don't know the parts) but can be ontological (the whole genuinely has new causal powers).

**Implication**: For agent collectives, this suggests that coordination patterns themselves — not just agent capabilities — can be the locus of new causal efficacy.
