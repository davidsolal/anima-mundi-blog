## Synthesis

Causal emergence is the formal description of how these higher-scale patterns become causally efficacious. Active inference provides the unifying computational framework, with spin glass models offering a concrete toy system for studying the transition from local to collective inference.

Future work should focus on empirical validation of these theoretical connections, particularly in LLM-based multi-agent systems where intuition-like heuristics may drive emergent coordination. The algebraic geometry approach may help identify when such emergent patterns are truly causal rather than epiphenomenal.
